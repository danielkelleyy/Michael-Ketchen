Frequently asked questions

Who is this event for?

Investors serious about building real estate strategies that create true time and financial freedom, whether you're buying your first door or scaling a portfolio. If you're ready for the next level and want the right room to get you there, this is for you.

What are the ticket tiers?

General Admission ($597): both main-stage days (Thu Oct 15 & Fri Oct 16), investor panels, breakout workshops by experience level and networking. VIP ($1,999, capped at 30): everything in GA plus front-row seating, meals, a VIP-only lunch with the speakers, session recordings, direct speaker access, small-group mastermind breakouts and the VIP Party. Speaker Day (+$999): an add-on exclusively for VIP holders, capped at 10.

An intimate add-on on Wednesday, Oct 14, the day before the Immersion kicks off. It's a $999 upgrade available exclusively to VIP ticket holders and capped at 10 seats.

The transformation happens in proximity. VIP is intentionally capped at 30 seats so you get real access to the speakers and a smaller-room mastermind experience. Once the 30 are gone, that's it for 2026.

Do I need experience to attend?

No. Breakouts are organized by level, from "zero to 6 doors" beginner tracks to advanced scaling and operations.

Where should I stay?

The Artisan Hotel and Tuscan Village in Salem, NH offer on-site lodging. Booking details and any room block will be shared after registration.

What's the refund policy?
