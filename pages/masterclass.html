Meet Michael Ketchen

I Didn't Have The Money. So We Learned How To Structure It.

I taught public school for 12 years. I'd just quit my $100K job when the credit union pulled our $2.5M loan on a 31-unit building. At the closing table.

Our equity was stuck, and we were losing money on the one deal we've ever lost money on. So we figured it out: SoFi personal loans, hard money, private money, seller carry. The next lender bet on us with a 26-unit in Keene, and we've done more than $30M in loans together since.

That's how we built our first $75 million of real estate, largely with private money, and kept all the equity. Today Commonwealth Collective manages more than $250M.

Tuesday night I'm showing you how we structure those deals.
